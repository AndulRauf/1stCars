1. YOUR ROLE & WHAT YOU OWN
----------------------------------------------------------------
As a Sales Associate you are the bridge between customers and the
1stCars marketplace. Your day-to-day work covers:

  - Test drive / virtual tour requests from customers
  - Buy-now enquiries, WhatsApp callbacks, cash-quote bookings
  - Follow-ups with leads until they are closed (won or lost)
  - Routing leads to the right people (inspectors, admin)

The system AUTO-ROUTES new leads to the sales associate with the
lowest current load and creates a follow-up task for you (due
within 24 hours). This is done by the Automation Center - you do
not need to "claim" leads manually, but you must act on them fast.


----------------------------------------------------------------
2. GETTING STARTED: LOGIN & FIRST LOOK
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the 1stCars website.
2. Click "Login" (top right).
3. Sign in with your work email and password. If you do not have
   an account yet, an administrator must create you one with the
   "Sales Associate" role - sign up on your own defaults to the
   "Buyer" role and will NOT give you staff access.
4. After login you are taken to "My Portal & Dashboards".

NOTE ON TEST/DEMO ACCOUNTS
For practice, the development team can provide demo accounts
(example: sales@1stcars.test, password Test@12345). Use them only
for training - never on the live site.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
  - "Welcome back, <your name>"
  - "Profile Role: Sales Associate Dashboard • Location: <city>"
  - A "Dashboard Hub" on the left with the full CRM menu:
        * CRM Overview
        * CRM Active Leads Desk
        * Follow-ups
        * Appointments
        * Test Drives
        * Sales Pipeline
        * My Car Listings
        * Upload New Car
        * Activities
  - A "Live System Alerts Hub" area on the right
  - Buttons: Refresh (reload data), Logout

3. YOUR DASHBOARD (ROLE HUB)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Default tab when you log in: "CRM Overview".

Left sidebar tabs (your full used-car sales CRM):
  - CRM Overview      : live KPIs (my cars, new leads, active
                        leads, follow-ups due, today's
                        appointments, upcoming test drives,
                        bookings and sales). Click any KPI to
                        jump to that screen.
  - CRM Active Leads Desk : all buyer inquiries routed to you,
                        with pipeline stage advance, claim,
                        detail panel, notes, follow-ups and
                        the activity timeline.
  - Follow-ups        : scheduled re-contact tasks (create,
                        complete, reschedule). The Automation
                        Center auto-creates one for every new
                        lead (due within 24 hours).
  - Appointments      : customer appointments calendar view.
  - Test Drives       : test-drive / virtual-tour requests and
                        their status (Scheduled / Confirmed /
                        Completed / Cancelled / No Show).
  - Sales Pipeline    : your leads grouped by stage
                        NEW -> CONTACTED -> QUALIFIED ->
                        APPOINTMENT -> TEST DRIVE ->
                        NEGOTIATION -> BOOKED -> SOLD (and LOST)
                        advance leads one click at a time.
  - My Car Listings   : the vehicles you uploaded, with their
                        listing status.
  - Upload New Car    : add a vehicle to inventory (admin must
                        approve before it goes live).
  - Activities        : chronological history of every lead
                        event and stage change (audit trail +
                        automation events).

Right panel:
  - Live System Alerts Hub
      - Shows unread notification badges
      - "Mark all as read" button
      - Each alert has a "Dismiss Alert" action

You can always press "Refresh" to pull the latest data from the
database. If anything looks missing, refresh before assuming a
lead is gone.

4. TEST DRIVE REQUESTS (CUSTOMER REQUESTS LOG)
----------------------------------------------------------------
What they are: customers who want a test drive / virtual tour of
a specific car.

Each request card shows:
  - Lead ID and customer name + mobile number
  - "Target Vehicle: <brand> <model>"
  - "Prefer Slot: <date and time>"
  - Status badge

Actions available on each card:
  - "Contacted"  -> marks the lead as contacted
  - "Resolve"    -> closes the request as handled

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Open "Customer Requests Log".
2. Work through the NEWEST requests first (or highest priority).
3. Call the customer using the number shown.
4. Confirm the test drive slot or arrange a virtual tour.
5. Press "Contacted" after you have reached the customer.
6. After the test drive/tour happens, press "Resolve".
7. Log any important notes so the next person has context.


----------------------------------------------------------------
5. CRM ACTIVE LEADS DESK
----------------------------------------------------------------
What they are: general buy queries, WhatsApp callbacks and
cash-quote bookings that are not tied to a test drive slot.

Each lead card shows:
  - Type badge: test drive (green), buy now (amber), other (sky)
  - Status pill: pending / contacted / resolved
  - Lead ID and creation date
  - Customer name, mobile, location/city
  - Preferred date and time
  - Target car (if any) and notes

Action available:
  - "Mark Solved" -> closes the lead

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Open "CRM Active Customer Leads".
2. Sort/read the lead card - note the type and status.
3. Contact the customer (call or WhatsApp).
4. Qualify the enquiry: are they serious? Which car? Budget?
5. After the conversation, update the lead:
      - Still talking/negotiating? Keep it open, follow up later.
      - Convinced / bought / requested test drive? Mark Solved.
6. If the customer wants a test drive, that request appears in the
   "Customer Requests Log" as well - handle it there too.

6. SALES PORTAL CONSOLE (ADVANCED)
----------------------------------------------------------------
There is a dedicated sales screen at the path /sales-portal
("CRM Sales Desk Console"). Use it for a fuller picture of your
pipeline.

It shows:
  - KPI metrics: Total Leads, Pending Review, In Discussion,
    Acquired / Closed
  - Filter tabs: All Database Leads, Pending, Contacted, Resolved
  - The same lead cards with more detail and notes
  - "Browse Inventory" button to look at cars while talking to a
    customer

Actions here (in addition to those on the dashboard):
  - "Mark In Discussion"  -> lead is being actively negotiated
  - "Mark Closed"         -> completed
  - "Delete Lead"         -> removes the lead permanently
       (only when truly a duplicate/junk - you will be asked to
        confirm before deletion)

TIP: Use "Mark In Discussion" when a deal is progressing so the
admin dashboard reflects real pipeline health.

7. NOTIFICATIONS & ALERTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
The system pushes notifications when:
  - A new test drive request is routed to you
  - A new buy-now / cash-quote lead is routed to you
  - A follow-up task is created (due in 24 hours)
  - An inspection on one of your deals is completed
  - An auction related to your lead ends

Workflow:
  1. Keep the "Live System Alerts Hub" open.
  2. Check unread badges first thing in the morning.
  3. Handle alerts in order of priority (urgent > high > normal).
  4. Dismiss only after you have acted on the alert.


----------------------------------------------------------------
8. EVERYDAY WORKFLOWS (STEP BY STEP)
----------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW A: NEW TEST DRIVE REQUEST
  1. Alert appears in the Alerts Hub / request in the log.
  2. Call the customer within 24 hours (automation expects this).
  3. Confirm the preferred slot and vehicle.
  4. Click "Contacted".
  5. After the visit/tour, click "Resolve".

WORKFLOW B: NEW BUY LEAD
  1. Lead appears in CRM Active Leads Desk.
  2. Call/WhatsApp, qualify budget + target car.
  3. If they want a test drive, direct them to the request flow.
  4. Update status as you go (Pending -> Contacted -> In
     Discussion -> Resolved).
  5. Hand over to admin to complete sale/payment once decided.

WORKFLOW C: FOLLOW-UP DUE
  1. The Automation Center creates a follow-up task for you.
  2. Complete the task with the outcome.
  3. If unresolved, schedule the next follow-up so nothing drops.

9. LEAD STATUSES & TERMINOLOGY
----------------------------------------------------------------
Lead types:
  - test drive  : customer wants to drive/tour a car
  - buy now     : customer wants to buy immediately / cash quote
  - other       : general enquiry (WhatsApp, form, etc.)

Statuses:
  - pending         : new, not yet contacted
  - contacted       : you have reached the customer
  - in discussion   : deal is being negotiated
  - resolved/closed : completed (won or lost)
  - assigned        : routed to a sales associate

Other terms you will see:
  - CRM        : Customer Relationship Management (lead tracking)
  - Lead       : a customer enquiry in the system
  - Assignee   : the staff member responsible for the lead
  - Follow-up  : a scheduled reminder/task to re-contact a lead


----------------------------------------------------------------
10. RULES TO ALWAYS FOLLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Act on new leads within 24 hours - automation assigns them to
   you precisely so they are handled fast.
2. Never delete a lead unless it is clearly a duplicate or junk.
3. Keep statuses truthful: don't mark "Resolved" before the
   customer is actually handled.
4. Never share customer mobile numbers outside the company system.
5. If a lead mentions an inspection or auction, involve the
   appropriate staff (inspector / admin) and note it.
6. Use "Refresh" before making decisions on stale-looking data.
7. Escalate any customer who is unhappy - do not let issues sit.


----------------------------------------------------------------
11. TROUBLE & HELP
----------------------------------------------------------------
- I can't see a lead: press Refresh. Check the filter tabs
  (Pending/Contacted/Resolved) - it may be in another tab.
- Login fails: contact your admin to confirm your account role is
  "Sales Associate" and your email is correct.
- Duplicate lead: mark one Solved with a note; do not delete both.
- Unsure about a customer's request: ask in the notes field so
  the next handler has full context.
- If the website shows "Mock Browser DB" instead of "Live
  Supabase", the app is running in demo/offline mode - data is
  only on that device and not shared.

For further help contact your Sales Manager or the Admin team.
