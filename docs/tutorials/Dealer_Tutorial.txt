1. YOUR ROLE ON 1stCars
----------------------------------------------------------------
1stCars auctions certified, 120-point-inspected cars to verified
dealers. You buy vehicles through LIVE dealer auctions at
wholesale-level starting prices.

To participate you must:
  - Have a Dealer account
  - Be VERIFIED (your dealer verification approved by admin)
  - Be added/eligible for each auction's dealer pool

Only eligible, verified dealers can see and bid on an auction.


----------------------------------------------------------------
2. GETTING STARTED: ACCOUNT & LOGIN
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Contact the 1stCars admin team to create your dealer account.
   Your profile role must be "Dealer" and your dealer verification
   must be approved, or you cannot bid.
2. Log in on the website.
3. You land on "My Portal & Dashboards" with the "Live Auctions
   Arena" tab open by default.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
  - Welcome message with your profile role
  - "Live Auctions Arena" - the list of auctions you are eligible for
  - "My Purchased Stock" - cars you have won and bought
  - Live countdown timers for each auction
  - Buttons: Refresh, Logout

NOTE ON TEST/DEMO ACCOUNTS
For practice, the team can provide demo dealer accounts (for
example dealer1@1stcars.test / dealer2@1stcars.test, password
Test@12345). Use demo accounts only for training.

3. ELIGIBILITY & VERIFICATION
----------------------------------------------------------------
The admin decides which dealers are eligible for each auction.
Your eligibility goes through stages:

  INVITED  ->  ELIGIBLE  ->  VIEWED  ->  BIDDED

  - INVITED    : admin has invited you to this auction
  - ELIGIBLE   : you are confirmed to bid
  - VIEWED     : you have opened/viewed the auction
  - BIDDED     : you have placed a bid
  - DISQUALIFIED : admin removed you from this auction

How to become eligible:
  1. Complete your dealer verification (admin side) - your account
     must show as verified.
  2. Admin adds you to the eligible dealer pool for the auction.
  3. Open the auction page - you will be marked as "viewed".

If you see no auctions, either your account is not verified yet or
you are not in any auction's eligible pool. Contact admin.


----------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE LIVE AUCTIONS ARENA
----------------------------------------------------------------
This is your home screen. It shows all auctions you are eligible
for, with:
  - Car details (brand, model, year, KM, fuel, transmission)
  - The inspection score (120-point report)
  - Starting bid and current highest bid
  - Minimum increment (how much each bid must go up)
  - A live countdown to the auction end
  - Reserve price indicator (if the reserve is met)

Before bidding, open the car and review the full 120-point
inspection report and photos. Bid only on cars you have studied.


----------------------------------------------------------------
5. HOW BIDDING WORKS (RULES)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Your bid must be at least the starting bid when there are no
   bids yet.
2. Once a bid exists, your bid must be at least:
       current highest bid + minimum increment
   Example: current bid Rs 10,00,000, increment Rs 50,000,
   your minimum bid is Rs 10,50,000.
3. Bids below the minimum are rejected by the system.
4. Only verified dealers in the eligible pool can bid.
5. A bid is binding. Winning is the start of a purchase, not an
   enquiry.
6. You cannot bid on an auction that has not started or has ended.

PLACING A BID (STEP BY STEP)
  1. In "Live Auctions Arena", open the auction you want.
  2. Review the car and inspection report.
  3. Enter your bid amount in the bid box.
  4. Confirm. The system checks eligibility, verification, minimum
     amount and the auction time window before accepting.
  5. A confirmation shows your bid was accepted and whether it
     extended the auction clock.

SAFETY (IMPORTANT)
  - Every bid you send has a unique request ID behind the scenes.
    If your internet fails and you retry, the system recognises
    the retry and does NOT place a double bid. Do not panic-click;
    the first successful bid is what counts.
  - Double-clicking the bid button does not double your bid.

6. ANTI-SNIPING & EXTENSIONS (READ CAREFULLY)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1stCars uses anti-sniping: if a bid is placed in the LAST 120
seconds of an auction, the clock EXTENDS by 120 seconds so other
dealers get a fair chance to respond.

Rules:
  - Each extension adds 120 seconds to the end time.
  - A limited number of extensions are allowed per auction
    (the admin configures this; default is up to 5).
  - When an extension happens, the auction status changes from
    LIVE to EXTENDED.
  - Keep bidding to the very end - a last-second bid does not lock
    the deal; it keeps the auction alive.

Strategy tip: decide your maximum price BEFORE the auction ends.
Chasing an ever-extending clock without a budget is how deals
become unprofitable.

7. BIDS, OUTBIDS & NOTIFICATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
You get automatic notifications:
  - "Auction Live"  : an auction you are eligible for has started
  - "You have been outbid" : someone placed a higher bid than
    your winning bid. You must bid again to stay in the race.
  - "Auction Won"   : you have the winning bid when it closes
  - "Payment Required" : the seller accepted; pay to complete

Bid history is shown in a MASKED format to everyone - you see the
amounts and timings, but never the other dealer's identity. You
can always recognise YOUR OWN bid (marked as yours).

If you are outbid:
  - Decide within the remaining time whether to place a new bid.
  - Remember the minimum: highest bid + increment.


----------------------------------------------------------------
8. WINNING AN AUCTION & PAYMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------
When an auction ends, it goes to the seller for review:

  Auction ends -> SELLER_REVIEW -> Seller ACCEPTS
               -> You are notified "Payment Required"
               -> Payment record created (PENDING)

Flow after you win:
  1. You receive the "Auction Won" notification.
  2. The seller reviews the result (they may accept or reject it).
  3. If the seller accepts, a payment is due for your winning bid
     amount. You are notified with the amount.
  4. Complete payment with the 1stCars team (the system tracks the
     payment as PENDING -> RECEIVED).
  5. Vehicle transfer is arranged; the car appears in your
     "My Purchased Stock".

If the seller REJECTS the result (reserve not met or seller
declines), you are notified and your winning bid is released - no
charges apply to you.

IMPORTANT: The highest bidder is not final until the seller
accepts. Do not resell or promise the car before payment is done.


----------------------------------------------------------------
9. MY PURCHASED STOCK
----------------------------------------------------------------
This tab shows the vehicles you have won and paid for. Treat this
as your showroom stock ledger:
  - Check it after every auction to confirm new purchases appear.
  - Keep your contact details current so dispatch can reach you.
  - Once delivered, you can list/resell those vehicles outside the
    auction platform.


----------------------------------------------------------------
10. AUCTION STATUSES EXPLAINED
----------------------------------------------------------------
  DRAFT         : being prepared by admin, not visible to dealers
  READY         : finalised, waiting to go live
  SCHEDULED     : a start/end time is set
  LIVE          : OPEN - you can bid now
  EXTENDED      : still open - the clock was extended by a late bid
  CLOSING       : being finalised by the system
  CLOSED        : finished
  SELLER_REVIEW : finished; seller deciding to accept/reject
  ACCEPTED      : seller accepted - payment due from winner
  REJECTED      : seller declined - winner released
  EXPIRED       : ended with no bids; vehicle returned to inventory
  CANCELLED     : admin cancelled it

You can ONLY bid on LIVE and EXTENDED auctions.


----------------------------------------------------------------
11. AUCTION ETIQUETTE & RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Only bid on cars you genuinely want - winning is binding.
2. Respect your maximum price. Do not overbid in the heat of the
   moment.
3. Never collude with other dealers to keep prices low - this gets
   all involved dealers banned.
4. Pay on time after the seller accepts. Repeated non-payment
   leads to suspension.
5. If your verification is pending, do not attempt to bid - fix
   verification with admin first.
6. Report technical issues (timer not moving, bid rejected
   wrongly) to admin immediately with the auction ID.


----------------------------------------------------------------
12. TROUBLE & HELP
----------------------------------------------------------------
- I can't see any auctions: your account may not be verified or
  you are not in an eligible pool. Contact admin.
- My bid was rejected: check the minimum (current + increment),
  whether the auction is LIVE/EXTENDED, and your verification.
- "Auction has ended" when I bid: the clock genuinely finished -
  the sale moves to seller review.
- Duplicate-looking bid after network error: it is NOT doubled;
  the retry returns your original bid. Check "My bids".
- Website shows "Mock Browser DB": the app is in demo/offline
  mode - bids there are NOT real. Contact admin before bidding.

For further help contact the Auction/Dealer support team.
