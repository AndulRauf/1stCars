1. YOUR ROLE & WHAT YOU OWN
----------------------------------------------------------------
You are the quality backbone of 1stCars. Every car listed for
sale is only trusted because it passed YOUR 120-point inspection.

Your responsibilities:
  - Conduct doorstep inspections of seller vehicles
  - Complete the 120-point report honestly and fully
  - Produce the overall vehicle score
  - Hand over the completed report so the vehicle can be certified
    and auctioned / published to the website

IMPORTANT: The Automation Center AUTO-ASSIGNS new inspection
requests to the inspector with the lowest current workload
(preference is given to an inspector in the same city). You do
not pick your own jobs - you work the jobs assigned to you.


----------------------------------------------------------------
2. GETTING STARTED: LOGIN & FIRST LOOK
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the 1stCars website.
2. Click "Login" and sign in with your work email and password.
   Only an administrator can create an account with the
   "Inspector" role. Signing up on your own gives the "Buyer"
   role and will NOT show you inspections.
3. You land on "My Portal & Dashboards" with the "Assigned Field
   Checklist" tab open by default.

NOTE ON TEST/DEMO ACCOUNTS
For training, the team can provide a demo inspector account.
Use demo accounts only for practice, never on the live site.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
  - "Welcome back, <your name>"
  - "Profile Role: Inspector Dashboard • Location: <city>"
  - "My Doorstep Inspection Worklist"
  - A button "Upload Report Card" on each assigned inspection
  - Buttons: Refresh, Logout

3. YOUR WORKLIST (ASSIGNED FIELD CHECKLIST)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Each assigned inspection card shows:
  - Inspection / lead ID
  - Seller name and mobile number
  - Vehicle brand, model, variant, year
  - Fuel and transmission
  - KM driven
  - City and full address (doorstep location)
  - Preferred date and time slot
  - Any seller notes
  - Status badge (see section 8)

Workflow:
1. Open "My Doorstep Inspection Worklist".
2. Review each assigned inspection's date, time and address.
3. Call the seller to confirm the appointment.
4. Plan your route so you are on time.
5. After the physical inspection, press "Upload Report Card".


----------------------------------------------------------------
4. PREPARING FOR A DOORSTEP INSPECTION
----------------------------------------------------------------
Before you leave:
  - Confirm the address and slot with the seller by phone.
  - Carry your device to fill the report (app/website) and take
    photos.
  - Know the vehicle basics: brand, model, year, variant, fuel,
    transmission, odometer reading.

During the visit:
  - Greet the seller, check the RC/ownership documents.
  - Physically inspect EVERY section - do not guess.
  - If something is damaged or modified, record it honestly.
  - Note the odometer reading and cross-check condition vs KM.

If the seller is not reachable or the address is wrong:
  - Do NOT submit a fake report.
  - Contact the admin/sales team and note the issue.


----------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE 120-POINT INSPECTION REPORT
----------------------------------------------------------------
"Upload Report Card" opens the 120-Point Inspection form. It is a
structured checklist with these major sections:

  - Engine
  - Exterior
  - Interior
  - Electrical
  - Tyres
  - Underbody
  - Scorecard

For each section you evaluate individual checklist points and mark
the condition found. The form automatically computes the OVERALL
SCORE at the end (out of 10 / percentage).

Scoring guidance:
  - Be consistent. The score directly affects whether the vehicle
    gets the "1st Mark" certification badge.
  - A low score is not a failure - it is an honest valuation.
    Dealers and buyers rely on your accuracy.
  - Do not inflate scores to please sellers or dealers. Your
    signature on the report is the company's reputation.


----------------------------------------------------------------
6. SUBMITTING THE REPORT CARD
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the report from your worklist.
2. Fill every section completely. Leave nothing blank.
3. Check the overall score is computed.
4. Review the whole report once before saving.
5. Submit / save the report card.

NOTE: Once submitted, the report is final unless an admin asks
you to correct it. Take your time BEFORE you submit.


----------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER YOU SUBMIT
----------------------------------------------------------------
When you submit a completed inspection report, the system does the
following automatically:

  1. The inspection status changes to "completed".
  2. The admin is notified that the report is ready.
  3. The vehicle becomes eligible to be published to the website
     as a certified car.
  4. The vehicle becomes eligible to enter the dealer auction
     (a base starting price is suggested based on the car's age).
  5. The customer (seller) sees the inspection result and score in
     their "Inspection Status" tab.

So a good, complete report directly keeps the sales engine
running. A late or incomplete report blocks listings and auctions.

Follow-up:
  - If the admin requests a re-inspection or fixes, update the
    report promptly.
  - If the seller asks questions about the score, explain how the
    points add up - do not change the score to satisfy them.

8. STATUSES & TERMINOLOGY
----------------------------------------------------------------
Inspection statuses:
  - pending     : request received, not yet assigned
  - assigned    : assigned to you, not yet inspected
  - completed   : 120-point report submitted
  - offered     : vehicle has been valued/offered
  - sold        : vehicle sold

Vehicle statuses you will see:
  - available : live on the website for buyers
  - listed    : ready for listing
  - bidding   : currently in a dealer auction
  - sold      : purchased
  - reserved  : on hold

Terms:
  - 120-Point Inspection : the full certification checklist
  - 1st Mark / Certified : badge a vehicle earns after passing
  - Overall Score        : the computed vehicle scorecard
  - Doorstep inspection  : inspection done at the seller's address
  - OBD / chassis check  : on-board diagnostics / chassis number
                           verification used during certification


----------------------------------------------------------------
9. QUALITY RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. NEVER submit a report for a car you did not physically inspect.
2. Fill all 120 points - partial reports are not accepted.
3. Be accurate, not generous. The score is a business-critical
   valuation used by dealers to bid.
4. Confirm appointments by phone; if you are going to be late,
   call the seller.
5. Keep vehicle/seller data private - never share it.
6. Report damage, accidents, repainting, mileage doubts and any
   red flags explicitly in the notes.
7. If you cannot complete a visit, escalate to admin - never fake
   a visit.
8. Keep photos and records consistent with the report.


----------------------------------------------------------------
10. TROUBLE & HELP
----------------------------------------------------------------
- No inspections assigned: press Refresh. Confirm your profile
  role is "Inspector" and your city is set correctly - automation
  prefers same-city inspectors.
- Can't open the report form: refresh the page and try again.
- Wrong address/slot: call the seller, confirm, and update admin.
- Car doesn't match the request (wrong model/reg): STOP, do not
  inspect a different car than assigned; report to admin.
- Website shows "Mock Browser DB": app is in offline/demo mode,
  data is local only - tell your admin.

For further help contact your Inspection Manager or Admin team.
